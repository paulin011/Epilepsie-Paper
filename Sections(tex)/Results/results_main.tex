\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This systematic review analyzed 13 primary studies published between 2013 and 2026. The evidence base comprises 9 detection studies and 4 forecasting studies with a total of 912 participants. Table~\ref{tab:detection-matrix} and Table~\ref{tab:forecasting-matrix} present the study matrices for detection and forecasting approaches, respectively. Table~\ref{tab:detection-architecture} and Table~\ref{tab:forecasting-architecture} provide architectural details, and Table~\ref{tab:metrics-summary} summarizes the metrics reported across studies.
