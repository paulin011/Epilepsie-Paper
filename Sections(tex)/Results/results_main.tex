\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This systematic review analyzed 13 primary studies published between 2013 and 2026. The evidence base comprises 8 detection studies and 5 forecasting studies with a total of 912 participants. Table~\ref{tab:1a} and Table~\ref{tab:1b} present the study matrices for detection and forecasting approaches, respectively. Table~\ref{tab:2a} and Table~\ref{tab:2b} provide architectural details, and Table~\ref{tab:3} summarizes the metrics reported across studies.
