# Study Characteristics

**Date:** 2026-01-22
**Topic:** Study Demographics, Designs, and Settings

---

## Overview

This systematic review analyzed **13 primary studies** (2013-2026) on wearable seizure detection and forecasting devices. The evidence base comprises **8 detection studies** and **5 forecasting studies** with substantial variation in sample sizes, study designs, and validation settings.

## Detection Studies (n=8)

| Study | Year | Design | Sample | Setting | Population | Seizures |
|-------|------|--------|--------|---------|------------|----------|
| **Spahr et al.** | 2025 | Prospective multicenter | n=384 | 8 EMUs | Adults/children | 49 CSs (test) |
| **Dong et al.** | 2026 | Prospective cohort | n=68 | Home | Adults | 1846 szs |
| **Reintjes et al.** | 2025 | Retrospective | n=120 | Real-world | Adults | 856 szs |
| **Wang et al.** | 2025 | Retrospective | n=28 | Hospital | Mixed (11 child, 17 adult) | 62 szs |
| **Fine** | 2025 | Phase 1 | n=15 training, 3 test | EMU | 3-46 years (median 19) | 70 tonic szs |
| **Elemam et al.** | 2025 | Cross-sectional | Q:198, HRV:30 | Hospital | Adults | Not specified |
| **Singh Rathore et al.** | 2024 | Case study | 1 patient | Home | Adult | 6-hour recording |
| **Borujeny et al.** | 2013 | 3-patient | n=3 | Hospital | Severe epilepsy | 20 szs |

**Key Observations:**
- **Spahr 2025** is the largest detection study (n=384) with prospective multicenter design
- **Dong 2026** has the highest seizure count (1846 seizures) from home monitoring
- **Singh Rathore 2024** is a single-patient case study with limited generalizability
- **Borujeny 2013** is the earliest study with only 3 patients

## Forecasting Studies (n=5)

| Study | Year | Design | Sample | Setting | Population | Data Duration |
|-------|------|--------|--------|---------|------------|---------------|
| **Vieluf et al.** | 2025 | Retrospective (Phase IV trial) | n=70 | Home | Median age 42.5 years | 5437 patient-days |
| **Meisel et al.** | 2020 | LOSO cross-validation | n=69 | Hospital | 28 female, mixed age | 2311.4 hours, 452 szs |
| **Stirling et al.** | 2021 | Retrospective + pseudo-prospective | n=11 | Home | Refractory epilepsy | Mean 14.6 months |
| **Nasseri et al.** | 2021 | Retrospective (temporal split) | n=6 | Ambulatory (RNS + E4) | Drug-resistant | 60+ days per patient |
| **Ode et al.** | 2023 | Retrospective | n=66 | Hospital | Focal epilepsy | 85 seizures |

**Key Observations:**
- **Vieluf 2025** has the largest forecasting sample (n=70) and longest data duration (5437 patient-days)
- **Nasseri 2021** has the smallest sample (n=6) but uses concurrent invasive EEG validation
- **Meisel 2020** employs rigorous LOSO validation on 452 seizures
- **Stirling 2021** has the longest monitoring duration (mean 14.6 months per patient)

## Evidence Base Limitations

1. **Small sample sizes:** 4/13 studies have n < 20
2. **Retrospective dominance:** Only 2/13 detection studies are prospective; all forecasting studies are retrospective
3. **Limited ambulatory validation:** Most studies conducted in controlled hospital settings
4. **Short duration:** Many studies have limited monitoring periods (days to weeks, not months)
5. **Single-patient studies:** 1 case study limits generalizability
